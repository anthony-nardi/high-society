# Ideas for later

Suggestions for making the games more fun, parked here until there is time.
Two have been done already: bot personas (names, avatars and tempers
chosen in the lobby) and the flag planted when a chain is founded in
Acquire.

## Across every game

- **Rematch streaks and grudges.** Keep a head-to-head record between the
  same people and show it in the lobby: "You lead Bot 2 four games to
  three." Builds on the stats already kept; gives every rematch a stake.
- **Game-over reveal with a story.** Replay the turning point on the
  results screen: the merger that paid out most, the bust that cost the
  game, the lion that flipped the wrong zebra. The logs already hold the
  data.
- **Taunts and reactions.** A small set of emoji reactions players can fire
  at the table, animated over their card.
- **Daily challenge.** One shared seed per day per game, so friends can
  compare a score against the same deal. Suits the drafting games and PUSH.
- **Replay scrubber** on the game-over screen that steps through the whole
  game move by move. Especially good for learning YINSH or GIPF.

## Acquire

- **Stock ticker.** Prices scrolling across the top, flashing when a chain's
  price jumps.
- **Mergers that flood.** The swallowed chain turning into the survivor's
  colour tile by tile along the board rather than all at once.
- **Last-lap badge.** When someone calls the end, a badge on each player as
  their final turn comes.

## PUSH

- **The crowd.** A rising tension note with each card pushed, a heartbeat at
  three full columns, and a record scratch on a bust.
- **Streak badges.** "Pushed six cards and lived" shown on the player card
  for the rest of the turn.

## Medici

- **The harbour.** Ships that visibly fill as lots are won, sail out at
  day's end and return empty; the ledger "weighs" each ship with a cargo
  animation.
- **Auctioneer's gavel.** A gavel sound and a quick "going once" beat before
  the auctioneer's final bid.

## Condottiere

- **Season weather over the map.** Winter frost or spring blossom washing
  over the battlefield when those cards are played.

## Ecosystem family

- **Living grids.** Animals that twitch or blink once placed, and at scoring
  time the cheetah's pounce and the lion's charge travelling across the grid
  to their prey before the flip.

## Bot personas, next steps

- Tempers currently change the judgement in Acquire, Medici and PUSH only.
  Candidates for dials: Condottiere (budget share), For Sale and High
  Society (bid ceilings), No Thanks! (chip patience), Fuji Flush (equity),
  the drafting games (room-to-grow weight).
